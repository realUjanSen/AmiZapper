    Chancellor's Message

    I wish you a productive year ahead, which will have tremendous personal growth for you.

    I am sure you enjoyed the break with family and friends and are all reenergized for campus.

    At Amity our aim and dream is to equip you and each Amitian with the right value system, caring nature for others and thorough professional knowledge so that you can be the leaders of our Country and the world who will change all this. 

    In this day and age, security and safety is very important.  As you know, at Amity we have always been very concerned about this.  There will be no University campus in India that will have turnstile access entry control, cctv cameras, security dogs, patrolling gypsies and so many security personnel.  Some students may feel inconvenienced with such strict measures but it is being done for your safety and I am happy that thousands of you cooperate patiently on a daily basis.

    If there are any recommendations you have to further increase your safety, or for that matter to improve anything in the University, you know that you can always reach me at my email id president@amity.edu
